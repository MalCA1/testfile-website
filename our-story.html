Our story

More play. More backyard.

Lucky Kids Sports is here to make it easier for kids to put down the screen and find the fun in moving, practicing, and playing outdoors.

Why we’re here

We believe active play can be joyful, simple, and part of an ordinary day. Our goal is to bring together kids’ sports gear that makes backyard time inviting, whether it’s skipping, trying a new footwork challenge, or kicking a ball around.

We’re preparing to launch in Australia. The store’s first announcement describes sturdy, kid-friendly gear selected for real backyard use. We’ll share more confirmed details as they’re ready.
